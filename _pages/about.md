I'm a Ph.D. student in Civil Engineering at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). My current research interests include Intelligent building control, semantic models (e.g., Brick), Control description language (CDL), Large language model, Robotics, SLAM, Machine learning. I'm currently supervised by [Prof. Zhe Wang](https://walterzwang.github.io/).

Prior to joining HKUST, I was an algorithm engineer at Meituan, Beijing from 2023 to 2024. I received my Bachelor and M.Phil at Nankai University, majoring in Intelligent Science and Robotics, supervised by Prof. Jing Yuan.
