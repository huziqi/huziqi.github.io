IBPSA Education Webinars are organized by IBPSA Education Committee, with the purposes of 1) to identify education and training needs throughout the building simulation performance community including academia, private sector and government; 2) to initiate, develop, encourage and/or coordinate new education material and methods; 3) offer training sessions to non-members as well as members; 4) promote the development and sharing of best practices in Builiding performance simulation education.

I'm honored to be invited to give a talk on AutoControl: an end-to-end fully automated workflow for control design of building energy systems.
