CUE2025 was organized by the renowned Applied Energy Innovation Institute (AEii) and International Centre of Urban Energy Nexus (UEX), the Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and Energy360. The CUE2025 was hosted by the University of Kitakyushu and supported by the international journals, Applied Energy, Advances in Applied Energy.

I'm honored to be invited to give a talk on AutoControl: an end-to-end fully automated workflow for control design of building energy systems. And a round table talk was conducted after the presetation.
