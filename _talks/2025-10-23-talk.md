BuildNext Seminar Series — a global research seminar that highlights emerging innovations in intelligent buildings is organized by Prof. Bing Dong from Syracuse University. Each week, BuildNext invites leading researchers to share their latest work and foster open discussions among senior PhD students and early-career researchers worldwide.
