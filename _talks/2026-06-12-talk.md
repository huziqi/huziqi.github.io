The International Conference on Building And Simulation (BAS) will take place at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in Hong Kong, China, from 11-13 June 2026. BAS2026 fosters collaborations among researchers, architects, environmental scientists, facility managers, and policymakers, focusing on energy efficient building and low carbon city solutions. I presented my work about Orthogonality-constrained basis driven model predictive control for few-shot adaptation in HVAC system control.
