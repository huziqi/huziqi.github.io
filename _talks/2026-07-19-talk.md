Nexus Forum 2026 was held at the University of Oxford from 19 to 22 July 2026. The forum was co-organised by The Hong Kong Polytechnic University and Cell Press, and co-chaired by Prof. Jerry Yan, Editor-in-Chief of *Nexus*, and Prof. Michael Obersteiner, Director of the Environmental Change Institute, University of Oxford. Themed "Unity in Diversity: Interdisciplinary Perspectives for a Sustainable Future", the forum brought together scholars from around the world to address challenges in sustainability, climate resilience and integrated systems.

I presented my work on a few-shot transferable control method for HVAC systems.
